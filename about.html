Our Mission

Too many people in Hyderabad want to run but never start — they think they're not fit enough, too busy, or don't know where to begin. Pavvathon Runners exists to change that.

We are one community, not a list of separate clubs. Our main goal is to motivate people to run — and we do that by organising events, sharing updates on this site, and creating a welcoming space where every pace is celebrated.

From KBR National Park to Necklace Road, Gachibowli to Banjara Hills — we bring runners together at events across the city. Just show up. We'll be there.

What We Believe

👟
Anyone can run

You don't need to be an athlete. If you can walk, you can run. Start with 100 metres if that's what it takes.

🐢
Slow is still running

Your pace doesn't matter. What matters is that you showed up. We celebrate every kilometre.

🌅
Consistency beats intensity

Three short runs a week beat one long run a month. Small habits create big change.

Why "Pavvathon"?

Pavvathon comes from the idea of a marathon of steps — a long journey made of small, consistent efforts. Just like running itself, it's not about one giant leap. It's about showing up every day and moving forward.

We chose this name because our mission is not about elite athletes or race podiums. It's about every person in Hyderabad who decides to run — and the one community that motivates them to keep going.
