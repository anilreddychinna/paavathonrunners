About Us

PAVVATHON is a community-driven running movement dedicated to inspiring healthier lives through meaningful running experiences.

Built around the 11.11 km Quarter Marathon, PAVVATHON encourages people of all ages and abilities to embrace progress through consistency, community, and purpose.

We believe that every step forward—no matter how small—has the power to transform individuals, strengthen communities, and create lasting impact.

More than an event organizer, PAVVATHON is a platform that brings together runners, walkers, volunteers, families, and fitness enthusiasts to celebrate personal milestones and collective achievements.

Every event is thoughtfully designed to create memorable experiences, foster meaningful connections, and inspire people to continue their journey towards better health and well-being.

At PAVVATHON, we don’t simply organize runs.
