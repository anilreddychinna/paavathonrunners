Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — simple and transparent.

Last updated: June 2026

Who We Are

Paavathon Runners is one running community in Hyderabad that motivates people to run and posts community events. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly.

What We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Event submissions: Event details, venue, date, and contact email when you post an event.
  • Contact form: Name and email if you reach out to us.
  • Newsletter: Email address if you subscribe for running motivation and event updates.

How We Use It

Your information is used solely for operating the Paavathon Runners website:

  • Displaying events on our events page
  • Responding to your messages
  • Sending newsletters and motivational content (only if you opt in)

We do not sell, share, or trade your personal information with third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers. No personal data is shared with advertisers.

Third-Party Services

We may embed Google Maps for event locations. These services have their own privacy policies that govern how they handle data.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at contact@paavathonrunners.in.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, reach out at contact@paavathonrunners.in.
